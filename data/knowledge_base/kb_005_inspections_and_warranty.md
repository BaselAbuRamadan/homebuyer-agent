# Inspections and warranties on new homes

New homes can still have defects. Buyers commonly arrange:
- **Pre-drywall inspection:** checks framing, plumbing, electrical and HVAC before walls are closed.
- **Final inspection:** before the builder walkthrough, so findings go onto the punch list.
- **11-month inspection:** shortly before a typical one-year builder warranty ends.

Check that the purchase agreement allows independent inspections; most builders permit them with scheduling notice.

**Builder warranties** often follow a "1-2-10" pattern: 1 year for workmanship and materials, 2 years for major systems (plumbing, electrical, HVAC), and 10 years for structural defects. Terms vary — read the actual warranty document and note how to file claims.
