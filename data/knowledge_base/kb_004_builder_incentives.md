# Understanding builder incentives

Builders frequently offer incentives instead of cutting list prices. Common types:

- **Closing cost contributions** — a fixed amount toward closing costs.
- **Interest rate buydowns** — the builder pays to lower your rate. A *temporary* buydown (e.g. 2-1 buydown) lowers the rate for the first year or two only; a *permanent* buydown lowers it for the life of the loan.
- **Price reductions** — most common on move-in ready ("spec") homes the builder wants to sell quickly.
- **Free upgrades** — appliances, flooring, landscaping.

Things to check:
- Is the incentive conditional on using the **preferred lender** or title company? Compare that lender's full Loan Estimate with an outside lender.
- Is a rate buydown temporary or permanent? Calculate payments after it ends.
- Does a price reduction affect the appraisal? Upgrades do not always add equal appraised value.
- Get every incentive written into the purchase agreement.
