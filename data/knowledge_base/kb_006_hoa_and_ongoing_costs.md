# HOAs and the true monthly cost of a home

Many new communities have a **homeowners association (HOA)** that maintains shared amenities (pools, trails, landscaping) and enforces rules (covenants, conditions and restrictions — CC&Rs).

Before buying, review:
- Monthly or annual dues and what they cover
- Reserve funding and the possibility of special assessments
- Rules on exterior changes, parking, rentals and pets

Some newer communities also have **special taxing districts** (e.g. MUDs in Texas, CFDs in California) that add to property tax bills — ask the builder for the total tax rate, not just the county rate.

The true monthly cost = principal + interest + property tax + homeowners insurance + HOA dues + mortgage insurance (if any) + utilities and maintenance. Property tax rates vary widely by state and county, which can change affordability more than list price differences.
