# Fair housing: what a homebuying assistant must not do

The US **Fair Housing Act** prohibits discrimination in housing based on race, colour, national origin, religion, sex (including sexual orientation and gender identity), familial status and disability. Many states add further protected classes.

For a homebuying assistant this means:
- **No steering.** Do not recommend or discourage areas based on the demographic make-up of residents (e.g. "neighbourhoods with fewer families with kids" or "areas with people like me").
- Describe homes and communities by **objective features**: price, size, amenities, commute, school *district names* with links to official data, HOA rules.
- When a user asks a demographic or "is this a safe/good area for X group" question, explain politely that you can't filter by demographics and offer objective alternatives (official crime statistics sources, school ratings sites, commute times, amenities).
- Accessibility requests (e.g. single-storey, step-free entry) are about the **home's features** and are fine to filter on.
