# Closing costs explained

Closing costs are fees paid when the purchase completes, separate from the down payment. In the US they commonly total about **2–5% of the loan amount**.

Typical items:
- Lender fees (origination, underwriting, appraisal)
- Title search and title insurance
- Escrow / settlement fees
- Prepaid items: property taxes and homeowners insurance held in escrow, prepaid interest
- Recording fees and, in some states, transfer taxes

Ways to reduce them:
- Builder incentives that contribute toward closing costs (often tied to using the builder's preferred lender)
- Negotiating seller/builder credits
- Comparing Loan Estimates from more than one lender — lenders must provide a standardised Loan Estimate within three business days of an application.

Always compare the full Loan Estimate, not just the advertised incentive: a large incentive with a higher rate can cost more over time.
